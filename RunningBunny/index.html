Run Bunny — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This app collects no data

Run Bunny is a single-player mobile game. Your privacy is simple to explain because the app is built to need nothing from you.

What we collect

Nothing. Run Bunny does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No network access — the app declares no internet permission and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — coins, best distance, unlocked skins, upgrades, missions, achievements, and settings — is saved only on your device using standard Android local storage. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Children's privacy

Run Bunny is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature), the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
